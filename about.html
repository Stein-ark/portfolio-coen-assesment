01

What I build

Most of my work is full-stack web and mobile. I take a product from data model and API through to the interface people actually use: schema design in MongoDB, REST endpoints on Node and Express, then React or Next.js on the front, or React Native and Expo when it needs to be an app.

That has meant a short-term rental platform with role-based authentication, a classroom access and ticketing system with approval workflows, a mobile app that talks to a Bluetooth Low Energy weighing scale, and live production sites for small businesses. I have also worked under contract as a frontend developer.

02

The engineering side

The degree is Computer Engineering, and the part of it I care most about is control systems and simulation. My final-year project, DroneAid, is a 3D quadcopter flight simulation built in Simulink and Simscape Multibody, running a full cascade control stack: inner angular-rate loops, outer attitude loops and altitude hold, flying an autonomous waypoint search-and-locate mission.

The gains were derived analytically from measured mass and inertia rather than tuned by hand. That distinction matters to me: I would rather understand why a system is stable than nudge sliders until it stops falling over.
